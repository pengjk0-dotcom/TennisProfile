# Third-Party Assets

TennisProfile depends on third-party datasets and models that are not
redistributed in this repository.

## THETIS

THETIS provides the RGB groundstroke videos used for the reference and group
analyses. Obtain the dataset from its official provider and follow its original
terms.

Reference:

S. Gourgari, G. Goudelis, K. Karpouzis, and S. Kollias. "THETIS: Three
Dimensional Tennis Shots a Human Action Dataset." CVPR Workshops, 2013.
https://doi.org/10.1109/CVPRW.2013.102

## SportsPose

SportsPose provides the independent 3D pose sequences and matched RGB streams
used for the cross-source checks. Access must be obtained from the dataset
authors under their distribution terms.

Reference:

C. K. Ingwersen, C. Mikkelstrup, J. N. Jensen, M. R. Hannemose, and
A. B. Dahl. "SportsPose: A Dynamic 3D Sports Pose Dataset." CVSports, 2023.

## 4D-Humans / HMR2

The HMR2 checkpoint and 4D-Humans implementation are obtained from the official
project. This repository records the base commit and checkpoint hash but does
not redistribute either asset.

Project: https://github.com/shubham-goel/4D-Humans

Reference:

S. Goel, G. Pavlakos, J. Rajasegaran, A. Kanazawa, and J. Malik. "Humans in
4D: Reconstructing and Tracking Humans with Transformers." ICCV, 2023.
https://doi.org/10.1109/ICCV51070.2023.01358

## SMPL

SMPL model files must be obtained under the original SMPL license. They are
not included in this repository.

Reference:

M. Loper, N. Mahmood, J. Romero, G. Pons-Moll, and M. J. Black. "SMPL: A
Skinned Multi-Person Linear Model." ACM Transactions on Graphics, 2015.
https://doi.org/10.1145/2816795.2818013
